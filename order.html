Select Your Strain

I usually start by using Weedmaps to find a local dispensary or delivery service with available strains.

To learn more about each strain’s effects, I recommend visiting Leafly. Just enter any strain name there, and you’ll get a helpful summary of its characteristics and effects.

Personally, I prefer Sativa or purple strains—something uplifting. Some of my favorites include Green Crack, Blue Dream, Gush Mints, Government Oasis, Jungle Juice, and Do Over.

Preparing Your Supplies

Once you’ve selected your strain and have your ounce (28 grams) ready, you’ll also need 1.5 liters of vodka.

Clean neutral vodkas work best; these days I use Vitalia.
