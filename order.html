Craft Your Perfect Bottle

Follow the simple steps below to begin your handcrafted tincture order.

Select Your Strain

I usually start by using Weedmaps to find a local dispensary or delivery service with available strains.

To learn more about each strain’s effects, I recommend visiting Leafly. Just enter any strain name there, and you’ll get a helpful summary of its characteristics and effects.

Personally, I prefer Sativa or purple strains—something uplifting. Some of my favorites include Green Crack, Blue Dream, Gush Mints, Government Oasis, Jungle Juice, and Do Over.

Preparing Your Supplies

Once you’ve selected your strain and have your ounce (28 grams) ready, you’ll also need 1.75 liters of vodka. I’ve tried brands like Grey Goose and SKYY, but I’ve found that a clean, neutral vodka works best. These days, I use Vitali for its smooth and pure flavor. I generally avoid Russian vodkas, as some can have a harsh, fuel-like aftertaste.

Order & Processing

Once you have your vodka and cannabis ready, please contact me. Most orders are filled within 2 days, though processing can take longer—especially around holidays. I’ll provide an estimated completion time when I pick up your supplies.

Large orders may require an additional deposit. Completed bottles must be retrieved within one month of ordering, or they may be forfeited. Payments are cash only—no exceptions (as you’d expect for cannabis-related transactions).

Each finished bottle will be clearly labeled with your strain and will contain only the alcohol and marijuana you provided. If you have any questions or concerns, don’t hesitate to reach out. Most transactions are quick, smooth, and straightforward.
